BEAN STUDIO

BEAN STUDIO is a private production tool used by one small creator team to prepare and publish videos on its own YouTube channels (XiaoDou Chinese, DouDou Mandarin Chinese, Bean What If, Nhà Bao Việc).

The tool uses the YouTube Data API only on channels owned by the team: it fills in titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, subtitles and playlists, schedules publishing, posts the team's own comments, and reads channel analytics. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
